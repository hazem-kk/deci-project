08 · WHY BUSLY?

A small transport problem
can create a big daily cost.

TIME

Missed buses become missed minutes.

When people cannot estimate arrival, they may wait too long, leave too late, or miss a connection.

EFFORT

Information is often scattered.

Stop signs, maps, messages and route information may not answer the same question in one place.

CONFIDENCE

Uncertainty changes behaviour.

A person who is unsure about the correct stop or direction may avoid public transport altogether.
