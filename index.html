Why Busly?

A small transport problem can cost a lot of time.

Students and everyday passengers often waste minutes just figuring out where to stand and which bus to trust. Busly turns that uncertainty into one clear answer.

Missed buses become missed minutes

Without a clear estimate, people wait too long, leave too late, or miss a connection entirely.

Information is scattered

Stop signs, paper timetables and route maps rarely answer the same question in one place.

Confidence changes behaviour

A rider unsure of the correct stop or direction may avoid public transport altogether — clarity brings them back.

Entrepreneurship Project

Built from a real opportunity

Problem
Students and bus users can struggle to know their nearest stop, which bus to take, when it arrives, and what to do next.
User
A student or everyday passenger who wants a quick, clear answer without digging through complicated transport information.
Solution
A simple bus-stop assistant combining nearby stops, route matching, live-style arrivals and a conversational helper.
Value
Less confusion. Less waiting uncertainty. Easier journey planning, from door to destination.
Safety & Privacy

Built to earn your trust.

Your location is only ever used to improve journey planning inside this prototype — nothing more.

Location, on your terms

Location access always requires explicit browser permission and is never assumed.

  • You can always type a starting point instead
  • Denying access never breaks the app

Minimal by design

No accounts, no passwords, no unnecessary personal information collected.

  • Saved stops stay in your browser only
  • Never asked for credentials or payment info

Honest simulation

This prototype uses realistic sample transport data unless a live transit API is connected.

  • Demo data is always clearly labelled
  • Buddy never claims sample data is live

This is a prototype, built to be tested

Stops, routes, arrival times and delays shown here are realistic sample data created for this entrepreneurship project. A production version would connect to an official live transit data source instead of simulating arrivals — this build focuses on proving the experience: finding a stop, planning a route, and getting a clear next step, fast.
